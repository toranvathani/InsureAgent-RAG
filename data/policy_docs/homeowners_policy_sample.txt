HOMEOWNERS INSURANCE POLICY — SAMPLE COVERAGE DOCUMENT

SECTION 1: FIRE AND SMOKE DAMAGE
This policy covers direct physical loss to the dwelling and personal property caused by
fire, lightning, or smoke, including damage from firefighting efforts. The deductible for
fire-related claims is $1,000. Coverage limit for dwelling fire damage is $250,000.

SECTION 2: WATER DAMAGE
This policy covers sudden and accidental water damage from burst pipes, appliance
malfunction, or storm-related roof leaks. It does NOT cover gradual water damage, seepage,
or flooding from natural bodies of water (flood coverage requires a separate policy).
Deductible for water damage claims is $1,500. Coverage limit is $100,000.

SECTION 3: THEFT
This policy covers theft of personal property from the insured dwelling, provided a police
report is filed within 48 hours of discovery. Claims for high-value items (jewelry, art,
collectibles) exceeding $10,000 require a prior appraisal on file with the insurer.
Claims filed within the first 30 days of policy inception are subject to additional fraud
review. Deductible for theft claims is $500. Coverage limit for jewelry/valuables is
capped at $5,000 unless a valuables rider was purchased separately.

SECTION 4: GENERAL EXCLUSIONS
This policy does not cover: intentional damage, wear and tear, pest infestation, business
property, or losses occurring while the property was vacant for more than 60 consecutive days.
