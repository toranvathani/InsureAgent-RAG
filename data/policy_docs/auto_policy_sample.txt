AUTO INSURANCE POLICY — SAMPLE COVERAGE DOCUMENT

SECTION 1: COLLISION COVERAGE
This policy covers damage to the insured vehicle resulting from a collision with another
vehicle or object, regardless of fault. Deductible for collision claims is $750. Coverage
is limited to the actual cash value of the vehicle at time of loss, up to a maximum of
$40,000.

SECTION 2: COMPREHENSIVE COVERAGE
This policy covers damage to the insured vehicle from causes other than collision,
including theft, vandalism, fire, hail, flooding, and animal strikes. Deductible for
comprehensive claims is $500. Coverage limit is $40,000, same as collision.
Claims for theft of the entire vehicle require a police report filed within 24 hours
of discovery.

SECTION 3: LIABILITY COVERAGE
This policy covers bodily injury and property damage the insured is legally responsible
for after an at-fault accident. Bodily injury liability limit is $100,000 per person /
$300,000 per accident. Property damage liability limit is $50,000 per accident. This
policy does NOT cover injuries to the insured driver — see Personal Injury Protection.

SECTION 4: UNINSURED / UNDERINSURED MOTORIST
This policy covers the insured's medical expenses and vehicle damage when the at-fault
party has no insurance or insufficient coverage. Limit matches the policy's liability
limits unless a lower limit was specifically elected at purchase.

SECTION 5: GENERAL EXCLUSIONS
This policy does not cover: damage from racing or other illegal driving activity, use of
the vehicle for commercial ridesharing or delivery without a commercial rider, damage
occurring while the vehicle was being driven by an unlisted driver not disclosed at
policy inception, or normal wear and tear. Claims filed more than 30 days after the date
of loss without a documented reason for the delay are subject to additional review.
